# Surface brief: GymTracker app (index.html)

Scope: whole PWA shell, four tabs (Hoy, Historial, Calendario, Progreso) plus Respaldo sheet. Mode: Operate. Audience: the owner, one-handed between sets on Android. Job: log a set in two taps; review history, attendance and progress at home.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every set is an attempt on the platform. The app is a powerlifting meet scoreboard, not a card list: the next attempt's weight dominates the screen, and logging it lights the judges' lights. Refuses the category default of stacked dark cards with small input fields.

OWN-WORLD: Scoreboard black #0E1116 ground, white figures, platform yellow #F2C230 for the primary action and records, red #E23B2E only for destructive or "no lift". Oswald condensed numerals at scoreboard scale, system sans for body. Three round referee lights as the recurring mark.

STORY: Open app, see today's session and the next attempt pre-filled from last time, nudge weight/reps with huge −/+, tap Anotar; lights flash white (yellow on a record). At home, scan history by date, see lit days on the calendar, follow max weight per exercise.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: date (tappable) and session count. Exercise chips row. Center: exercise name, three lights, weight at ~120px with −/+ 64px buttons, reps below. Today's sets list. Yellow "Anotar serie" 64px bar above a 4-tab bottom nav.
